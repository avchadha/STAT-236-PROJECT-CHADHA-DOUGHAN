Each detection configuration uses 1,500 independent null calibration matrices and 600 fresh null and 600 planted matrices per signal level. The combined sum-or-scan test splits the 5% level between its components. Recovery uses 600 additional planted matrices per signal level and requires both index sets to match. Bands and intervals are marginal Wilson 95% intervals, conditional on the calibration cutoff. Methods share a matrix within each trial. Omitted methods were not computed; exact scans are never replaced by approximations.
